SpookyStories Uploader

這是 YouTube 頻道「SpookyStories」的內部製作工具，僅供頻道擁有者本人使用，不對外提供服務。

它會把製作完成的恐怖故事朗讀影片上傳到頻道擁有者自己的 Google 雲端硬碟供審核，並在擁有者核准後上傳到其 YouTube 頻道。

SpookyStories Uploader is a private production tool used only by the owner of the SpookyStories YouTube channel. It uploads finished narration videos to the owner's own Google Drive for review and, after the owner approves, to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.
